# Metadata lifecycle checkpoint

The seven supplied 88f0dc7 regressions are maintained as SessionMetadataLifecycleTest, with an additional repeated closed-object regression. SessionMetadataTransactionBindingTest covers same-query borrow, foreign query, same/different-query REQUIRES_NEW, failed inner setup, physical-holder replacement, setup/rollback/unknown cleanup and reuse after completion.

Failure cleanup clears rows and refunds before registering the original RuntimeException; completed Work can register failure without regaining proof. A preallocated, stackless Error adapter records its original Error as cause; direct callers still receive the exact Error. Public poisonProof retains its original active-Work contract. Closed-object rejection uses preallocated per-Work objects and charges each attempt, including already-failed owners. The failure-only counter adds no Map/boxing/new exception and cannot reopen a failed query. Its units join cumulative operations; per-group counters do not allocate new bookkeeping after the first failure.

The metadata identity channel is a private Spring transaction resource installed by the actual ConnectionSettings. Each lookup recovers a retained identity without allocating, charges64, then checks Work/query/token, live synchronization/actual transaction, original DS ConnectionHolder and physical connection. It grants no business authority. Suspend removes the ancestor, resume restores the exact owner; completion clears aliases and unbinds once. Same-query inner transactions retain their distinct ConnectionSettings keys independently.

Source request/refund CSV and object/provider timeline are indexes, not full heap proofs. javap of packaged Spring7.0.9 is preserved in the delivery evidence: getResource has no synchronization snapshot, sorting, list or iterator allocation. The initial nonallocating root read is charged immediately after recovering its actual payer; no new allocation precedes that recovery. These limits must stay visible in independent review.
